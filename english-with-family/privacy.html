PLEASE READ BEFORE USING THE APP

Your privacy matters

This notice describes the current version of English With Family, an educational English-practice project. It is general information, not a promise that every third-party browser or hosting service behaves identically.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores learner profile names, lesson completion, practiced phrases, practice dates and related progress in your browser's local storage. The app does not currently provide user accounts or an app-operated cloud sync service.

Anyone with access to the same browser profile or device may be able to see or change this local progress. Avoid entering sensitive information. You can use a nickname instead of a full name and use the app's reset control to clear a learner's progress.

2. Microphone and speech features

Microphone access is optional. The app uses browser speech recognition when available and only attempts to start recognition after you press the speaking control. Browser speech recognition may send audio or transcripts to the browser provider or another service according to that provider's implementation and settings. We cannot promise that speech recognition is processed only on your device. Check your browser's privacy settings and do not speak sensitive or confidential information.

Text-to-speech reads practice phrases aloud. Availability and processing depend on your device and browser.

3. Hosting and network requests

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical information as described in GitHub's own policies. The app itself has no app-operated account server, analytics system, advertising system or remote progress database in this version.

4. Children and family use

The app is designed for family learning, but it is not a verified child-safety service. A parent or guardian should supervise younger children, especially when granting microphone permission. Do not enter a child's full name or other sensitive personal details.

5. Your choices

  • You can use a nickname.
  • You can decline microphone permission and type answers instead.
  • You can reset locally stored progress using the app's reset control.
  • Clearing site data in your browser also removes locally stored progress.

6. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when app features or data practices change. Before adding accounts, analytics, cloud sync or AI services, this notice must be reviewed and updated. For questions, use the contact method listed on the project's GitHub repository; do not send sensitive personal information.

Important: This is a practical starter notice, not legal advice or a certification of compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or other laws. Obtain legal review before a public or commercial launch, particularly if children use the app.
